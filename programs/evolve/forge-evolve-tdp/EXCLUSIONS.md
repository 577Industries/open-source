# Publication Boundary & Exclusions (`forge-evolve-tdp`)

All CAD/STEP files (`benchmarks/AF_ACP_7740_Bulkhead.stp`) and PMI tolerances in this directory are 100% synthetic unclassified geometry. They are loosely patterned on the public NIST MBE PMI validation test cases (`CTC-01..05`, `FTC-06..11`), but no NIST test case is included and none has been run. Zero ITAR, CUI, or export-controlled defense articles are present; example identifiers, markings and key URIs in `schemas/` are placeholders.
