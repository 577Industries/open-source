# Publication Boundary & Exclusions (`forge-kinetic-mbd`)

All CAD/STEP fixtures (`benchmarks/AF_ACP_7740_Bulkhead.stp`) and the robot controller programs printed by `forge-evolve plan-path` are derived from synthetic unclassified geometry. No NIST MBE PMI test case is included and none has been run, and no controller program files are committed. Zero ITAR, CUI, or export-controlled defense articles are present; example identifiers, markings and key URIs in `schemas/` are placeholders.
